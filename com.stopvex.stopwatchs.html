SPSM Plus

com.stopvex.stopwatchs
Data Collection & Privacy

SPSM Plus is a lightweight stop-watch and timer utility designed to operate entirely on your local device. We respect your privacy by design. This application does not collect, track, monitor, or upload any personal data, usage statistics, or diagnostic information to external servers.

Permissions & Security

The application does not request access to your device's location, contacts, phone, or sensitive storage. It operates strictly in isolation within the Android sandbox. No data is stored in the cloud. All user inputs (start/stop times, label names) remain exclusively on your device and are only used to render the UI in the foreground.

Third-Party Services

SPSM Plus does not integrate with third-party advertising networks, analytics SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or Amplitude), or cross-device tracking identifiers. There are no embedded web views loading external content, and no background data transmission occurs when the app is idle or closed.

User Control

You have absolute control over the data associated with this app. If you wish to reset all stopwatch or timer states, or remove any locally saved labels, you can do so directly within the application. Uninstalling the app will permanently remove all local data associated with it from your device.
